On taking the long way home, following your curiosity, and finding meaning in the margins.
THE MARGIN JOURNAL   ·   4 MIN READ

Some of the best things arrive without an invitation. A book left on a train. A conversation that changes your afternoon. We call it serendipity, this small talent for discovering something lovely when we were looking for something else.

Perhaps curiosity is simply a willingness to linger. To notice an unfamiliar word, a quiet garden, or a lucid thought that makes everything feel a little clearer.

Leave space for the unexpected.

There is no need to understand everything at once. Sometimes one word is enough to open a door. The world is full of ordinary moments waiting for our attention.

A slow walk can become a small adventure. A familiar street can reveal a new detail. And a page can take you somewhere you never intended to go.
